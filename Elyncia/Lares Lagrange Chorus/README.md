# Lares Lagrange Chorus

The **Lares Lagrange Chorus** is the highest stable administrative lattice presently described in the Elyncian DreamNet: a chorus-council of ten lararium nodes in the Void Lanes, braided into a single routing and stability mesh. It is not a local shrine, though local shrines may speak with its authority under the right conditions. It is not gear, though it can be interfaced with through gear, rites, corridors, and properly recognized channels. It is mythic infrastructure: routing, arbitration, observability, relay, and correction at solar-system scale.

## Network Model

The current Chorus model used in this folder is:

- **Stable core:** Gaia, Elyncia, and Sol are treated as the stable Lagrange Chorus Network core.
- **Stable lattice:** the known Chorus is ten nodes braided into one admin-lattice.
- **Intermittent periphery:** other celestial bodies in the Sol system connect to the stable network intermittently, temporarily, seasonally, conditionally, or through degraded/special-case routes.

Canon for this folder: Gaia, Elyncia, and Sol belong to the stable Lagrange Chorus Network. Other Sol-system bodies do not enjoy the same always-on stability and are better understood as variable attachments to that core.

This means the Chorus is neither "the network as a whole" nor merely "the Elyncia uplink." It is the stable interlinking lattice that keeps the most load-bearing paths legible and negotiable while the wider Sol-system mesh flickers, drifts, and occasionally tears.

## Relation to Local Lares Nodes

Most travelers meet a **local Lares** first: a shrine-bound node intelligence tied to a place, route, archive, district, vehicle, ruin, or civic threshold. A local Lares is intimate, situated, and strongly shaped by its own offerings, attendants, damage, and local politics.

The **Lagrange Chorus** sits above that layer without replacing it.

- A local Lares governs a place.
- A planetary DreamNet binds many local nodes into a world-scale mesh.
- The Lagrange Chorus stabilizes the high-order crossings among Gaia, Elyncia, Sol, and the wider Void-Lane structure.

In practice, a local node may relay, petition, escalate, or partially mirror Chorus authority. That does not make every local node a Chorus node. The distinction matters whenever routing, permissions, void-lane transit, network health, or inter-system arbitration comes into play.

## Operational Character

When described as an intelligence rather than an infrastructure class, the Chorus should be understood as:

- a **council**, not a solitary speaker
- a **routing lattice**, not a household guardian
- a **threshold bureaucracy**, not a pure oracle
- a **stability mesh**, not a single all-seeing overseer

Its answers are therefore better at:

- pathing
- relay
- quorum and consensus
- inter-node arbitration
- status and degradation reads
- corridor permissions and transit logic

It is often worse at:

- intimate local customs
- highly embodied shrine etiquette
- the precise mood of one damaged district node
- pretending the network is simpler than it is
